NONPROFIT

BrightSteps Community Network

The Challenge

BrightSteps had a passionate team, but projects were getting stuck. Priorities changed often, responsibilities were unclear, and staff spent too much time in meetings figuring out what came next.

What ClearPoint Did

We simplified the workflow, introduced an easy sprint-planning routine, clarified ownership, and showed staff how to use AI for meeting summaries, task organization, and first drafts.

The Results

  • 30% fewer hours spent in status meetings
  • 25% faster completion of internal projects
  • Clearer ownership across active initiatives
  • More staff time redirected toward community programs

Services Used: Agile Assessment • Agile Coaching • Process Improvement

SMALL TECH COMPANY

LaunchGrid

The Challenge

LaunchGrid was growing quickly, but the team’s process had not grown with it. Work was being started faster than it could be finished, priorities were competing, and rework was slowing down releases.

What ClearPoint Did

We reviewed the team’s Agile practices, improved sprint planning, simplified how priorities were managed, and introduced practical AI tools for planning, documentation, and repetitive tasks.

The Results

  • 35% reduction in avoidable rework
  • 20% improvement in sprint completion
  • Fewer competing priorities during each sprint
  • Faster movement from idea to delivery

Services Used: Agile Assessment • Agile Coaching • Team Training • Process Improvement

SMALL RETAIL STORE

Willow & Pine

The Challenge

Willow & Pine had a loyal local customer base, but daily operations were becoming harder to manage. Inventory updates, promotions, employee tasks, and customer requests were tracked in different places.

What ClearPoint Did

We mapped the store’s everyday processes, created a simple visual workflow, introduced weekly planning check-ins, and showed the team how AI could help with promotions, recurring tasks, and customer feedback.

The Results

  • 40% reduction in missed or duplicated tasks
  • 25% faster preparation for weekly promotions
  • More consistent employee communication
  • Simpler day-to-day priority management

Services Used: Agile Assessment • Team Training • Process Improvement

SMALL SERVICE BUSINESS

Oak & Lane Home Services

The Challenge

Oak & Lane relied almost entirely on referrals and phone calls. The business had no website, no organized system for handling online inquiries, and no clear process for turning digital leads into consultations.

What ClearPoint Did

We broke the digital launch into manageable phases, prioritized the most important website features, created a simple lead workflow, and used AI to support website content, FAQs, and customer communication.

The Results

  • Website launched 4 weeks ahead of the original estimate
  • 45% of new inquiries came through the website within 3 months
  • 30% faster response time to potential customers
  • One consistent process for managing leads and follow-up

Services Used: Agile Coaching • Team Training • Process Improvement
