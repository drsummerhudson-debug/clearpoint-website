PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy, explained simply.

This page explains how information would be handled on the ClearPoint website.

Last Updated: September 2026

ClearPoint respects your privacy and aims to keep things simple.

This website is a portfolio demonstration site created to showcase web design and business consulting concepts. ClearPoint is not currently operating as a real consulting business through this website.

Information We May Collect

If this site were used as a live business website, visitors could choose to provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Business name
  • Service of interest
  • Preferred consultation date
  • Preferred time of day
  • Information included in a message or consultation request

How Information Would Be Used

Information submitted through the website would be used to:

  • Respond to questions
  • Follow up on consultation requests
  • Understand what type of support a visitor is interested in
  • Improve the website experience

We would not sell personal information.

Cookies and Analytics

A future live version of this website may use basic analytics or cookies to understand how visitors use the site. If these tools are added, this policy would be updated to explain what information is collected and how it is used.

Third-Party Services

The website may eventually connect with outside services such as scheduling tools, contact forms, analytics platforms, or email providers. Those services may have their own privacy policies and practices.

Your Choices

You may choose not to submit personal information through the website. If you have already submitted information and would like it corrected or removed, contact:

hello@clearpointco.com

Portfolio Disclaimer

ClearPoint is a fictional business concept created for portfolio and educational purposes. Any client names, case studies, results, services, and business scenarios shown on this site are examples created for demonstration purposes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@clearpointco.com.
